# Figures

This directory retains the figures produced during the final manuscript analysis. Historical
subdirectory names (`app_1`, `app_2`, `sim_1`, and `sim_rev`) are kept because the output-free
notebooks refer to them directly. The current paper PDF in `paper/` is the authoritative source
for figure numbering and captions.

The primary final-application assets are under `app_2/`; final supplementary simulation assets are
under `sim_rev/`. Earlier variants remain available for provenance rather than being silently
discarded.
